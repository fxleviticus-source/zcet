How to Apply

Application Process

1

Deposit the Application Fee

Deposit the non-refundable K50 Application Fee and collect an Application Form at the Admissions Office.

2

Submit Your Application

Submit your completed Application Form and wait for an official admission notice with further guidelines.

Specific intake dates and application deadlines change between enrolment periods. Contact ZCET directly by phone or email to confirm the current application window before applying.
Entry Requirements

Requirements by Qualification Level

Certificate Programmes

Minimum of a G9 Certificate plus any 3 O-Level subjects.

Diploma Programmes

Minimum of a G12 Certificate or GCE with five Credits or better in English Language, Mathematics, Science plus any other two subjects.

ZCET's FAQ page also describes slightly different requirement breakdowns depending on the awarding body (Zambia Counselling Council vs. an affiliate university) for some diploma routes. Confirm the exact requirement for your chosen programme directly with ZCET's admissions office.

Scholarships & Student Support

Student Support Scheme

ZCET has partnered with individuals and organisations to offer student support to deserving, vulnerable candidates. Candidates are selected by a committee after careful evaluation of a vulnerability report. Deserving students may receive up to a 100% scholarship for an academic term, semester, year, or the full course.

Internship & Industrial Experience

Bridging Students to Industry

Zambezi College of Education and Technology endeavours to be an effective link between the community and the industry, ensuring that students are not only trained adequately but also provided with opportunities for industrial experience, networking and enterprise.
