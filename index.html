About ZCET

A College Built for an Underserved Region

This facility is meant to be a service among the most educationally excluded communities in Zambia, as around five neighbouring districts of North Western Province have remained without another higher education facility since independence over 50 years ago. Income generated is re-invested in the college to expand its operations, offer more training programmes and reach more vulnerable youths in the district and surrounding areas.

As a dedicated education and technology training facility, ZCET teaches a variety of classes spanning introductory, intermediate and advanced levels, with students graduating with accredited certificates and diplomas.
