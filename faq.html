Frequently Asked Questions

Answers sourced directly from ZCET, organised by category.

Registration & Accreditation

ZCET is a newly registered private college run by the North Western Development Foundation Limited. As published by the college, ZCET has been processing affiliation with the Zambia Counselling Council for certificate and diploma programmes in counselling-based fields, and has also been in the process of acquiring accreditation with the Higher Education Authority, alongside possible affiliation with Gideon Robert University.

Note: registration and accreditation status can change. Contact ZCET directly to confirm the current status before enrolling.

Programmes

Certificate programmes: minimum of a G9 Certificate plus any 3 O-Level subjects.

Diploma programmes: minimum of a G12 Certificate or GCE with five Credits or better in English Language, Mathematics, Science plus any other two subjects.

ZCET's published FAQ content also describes a Zambia Counselling Council-specific breakdown for some routes, which may vary slightly by affiliation. Confirm the exact requirement for your chosen programme with ZCET's admissions office.

As published by ZCET, TEVET courses were expected to be introduced at the college on both full-time and distance-learning mode once the registration process with the relevant authorities was completed. This announcement referenced a target intake date that has since passed, so it should not be treated as current.

Contact ZCET directly for the latest update on TEVET course availability.

Fees & Admissions

The application fee is a non-refundable K50, paid to collect an Application Form at the Admissions Office.

ZCET's published materials referenced a specific past intake deadline that is no longer current. Contact ZCET directly by phone or email to confirm the current application window.

At least 50% of fees is due as a first instalment on or before first reporting for the term/semester. The outstanding balance must be cleared at least 30 days before the closing date of the term/semester. Instalments do not apply to the application fee or lodging/accommodation fees.

Student Support

ZCET has partnered with individuals and organisations to offer student support to deserving, vulnerable candidates. Candidates are selected by a committee after careful evaluation of a vulnerability report. Deserving students may receive up to a 100% scholarship for an academic term, semester, year, or the full course.

Internships

ZCET endeavours to be an effective link between the community and the industry, ensuring that students are not only trained adequately but also provided with opportunities for industrial experience, networking and enterprise.
