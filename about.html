About ZCET

Zambezi College of Education and Technology is a project of the North Western Development Foundation, established to bring higher education and technology training to an underserved region of Zambia.

Our Background

A Facility Built for the Most Educationally Excluded

This facility is meant to be a service among the most educationally excluded communities in Zambia, as around five neighbouring districts of North Western Province have remained without another higher education facility since independence over 50 years ago.

Income generated from the college's operations is re-invested to expand training programmes and reach more vulnerable youths in Zambezi, Kabompo and the surrounding districts. As a dedicated education and technology training facility, ZCET teaches introductory, intermediate and advanced-level classes, with students graduating with accredited certificates and diplomas.

North Western Development Foundation

The Foundation Behind ZCET

Zambezi College of Education and Technology is a project of the North Western Development Foundation (NWDF), the organisation responsible for establishing and running the college.

Registration & Affiliation

Where ZCET Stands Today

ZCET is a newly registered private college run by the North Western Development Foundation Limited. As published by the college, ZCET has been processing affiliation with the Zambia Counselling Council for certificate and diploma programmes in counselling-based fields, and has also been in the process of acquiring accreditation with the Higher Education Authority, alongside possible affiliation with Gideon Robert University.

Registration and accreditation status can change over time. Contact ZCET directly for the most current status of any affiliation or accreditation.
